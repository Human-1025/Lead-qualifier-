# LeadQualifier — Full Design Strategy

## PHASE 0: Research Synthesis

### Market Reality
- US roofing contractors spend $1,500+/mo on Angi/HomeAdvisor
- Angi sells every lead to 3-5 competitors simultaneously
- 50%+ of leads are tire-kickers, wrong numbers, or "just wondering"
- Average contractor wastes 15-20 min per cold call = lost roofing revenue
- r/Roofing is filled with fury about Angi's business model

### Competition Gaps
| Competitor | Weakness | Opportunity |
|------------|----------|-------------|
| Angi/HomeAdvisor | Sells same lead to competitors, per-lead pricing | Flat rate + exclusive leads |
| Thumbtack | Pay per quote, no qualification | AI pre-qualification |
| JobNimbus/Housecall Pro | CRM tools, no lead qualification | Add intelligence layer |
| Manual qualification | Roofer calls every lead themselves | 24/7 automated AI |

### Target Customer (Primary Persona)
- US roofing contractor, 1-10 crews
- Currently using Angi/HomeAdvisor and frustrated
- Spends more time on the phone than on roofs
- Tech-savvy enough to use a dashboard, not to build one
- Pain: "I'm paying for leads my competitors also get"

## PHASE 3: Visual Design Strategy

### Brand Attributes
- Precise, premium, no-nonsense
- Dark theme with emerald/teal accent (roofing = money, green = growth)
- Linear-inspired minimalism — content emerges from darkness
- Professional but not corporate

### Design Tokens (from stunning-frontend-design skill)
- Background: near-black (`oklch(0.145 0 0)`)
- Primary accent: emerald-teal (`oklch(0.746 0.16 167.59)`)
- Typography: Inter, weight 400/510/590
- Borders: ultra-thin semi-transparent
- Shadows: none — use background luminance stepping

### Page Layout (Z-Pattern, F-Pattern scan)
- Hero: centered, single column, max-width constrained
- Pain section: 3-column stat cards
- How It Works: 3-column feature cards
- Features grid: contiguous grid with gap-px borders
- Pricing: centered single card (for now, single tier) with anchor comparison
- Final CTA: minimal, single message
